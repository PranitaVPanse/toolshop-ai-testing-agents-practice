# TS-1 Customer checks out the items in the cart

**Type:** Story
**Status:** Ready for test

## Story

As a customer
I want to check out the items in my cart
So that I can buy the tools I need

## Acceptance criteria

1. From the cart, the customer can proceed to checkout and sees the items, quantities, unit
   prices and the cart total.
2. The customer can change the quantity of an item or remove an item in the cart, and the total
   is updated.
3. A customer who is logged in continues to the billing address step. The address from their
   profile is pre-filled and can be changed for this order.
4. All billing address fields are required before the customer can continue.
5. The customer chooses a payment method and enters the details that method needs.
6. After confirming the payment the customer sees a confirmation with an invoice number.
7. The order is listed under "My invoices" in the customer's account with the same total.
